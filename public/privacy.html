Privacy Policy / سياسة الخصوصية

Listing Optimizer ("we") helps sellers write product listings. This policy explains what we collect.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, used only to sign you in and manage your subscription and free tries.
  • The product names, details and images you submit, which are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate listings.
  • Your plan, plan end date and number of free tries used.

How we use it

Only to run the service. We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, except the processors needed to run the service: Supabase (accounts and database), Render (hosting) and Google (sign-in and Gemini).

Payments

We do not collect card details. Payments are made by you through PayPal, Binance Pay or Orange Cash and confirmed manually via WhatsApp.
